OUR STORY

About Lanka Explorer

We built Lanka Explorer as our five-week web development mini project. Our goal is to help visitors discover Sri Lanka's heritage, beaches, wildlife and nature with a simple and friendly website.

OUR IDENTITY

Discover Sri Lanka with Lanka Explorer

Our logo brings together a compass, green mountains and tropical leaves — symbols of exploration and Sri Lanka's natural beauty.

From ancient heritage to scenic hills and beautiful coastlines, Lanka Explorer helps you plan your journey around the island.
